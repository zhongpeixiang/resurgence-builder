    Frequently Asked Questions

    How does the Build Lab detect synergies and set bonuses?

    Your selections are cross-referenced against the site's verified game database in real time. When the engine spots stat interactions — like Received Healing feeding Damage Reduction through a talent trigger, or a gear set 4-piece amplifying your OS Protocol — it flags them as active synergies. Conflicts and wasted stats are surfaced the same way, before you farm a single piece.

    Is the loadout data verified and up to date?

    Every entry is checked manually against the live game client, not scraped from wikis. The database behind the Lab currently holds 16 gear sets, 69 weapon talents, 66 OS Protocols, and 90 skill mod combos, each carrying its own last-verified patch label.

    Can I share my builds with other players?

    Yes — the entire build state is compressed into the URL hash. Hit Copy Build Link and anyone who opens it sees your exact loadout: gear sets, talents, weapons, OS Protocol, skill mods, and bonus attributes. No account, no sign-up.
